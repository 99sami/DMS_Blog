# Write a first draft of an artist statement for each of your projects:

--- 

Assignment 1 -  Video diary


My video concept explores the duality in my life, contrasting a calm, introspective side with a busy, public persona. In blending sound with visuals, I aimed to enhance this dynamic, using softer audio for the quieter moments and energetic sound to reflect the shift in tone. This fusion of sound and imagery allowed me to clearly tell the story of who I am, both as Sam and as Persia, someone deeply connected to queer culture particularly within the Ballroom scene. 
Through this visual narrative, I express not only my personal evolution but also my connection to myself and my chosen family. This has  shaped the project. Ballroom has profoundly shaped my identity and purpose, and I envision it continuing to be a pivotal part of my journey. Through this assignment I hope to one day give back to the community that has given me so much by representing them in new spaces, helping to preserve and grow this culture for future queer youth to discover and embrace.

--- 

Assignment 2 - Graphic icons 

'Mental Health & Wellness Retreat' encapsulates the essence of my design concept. I created 18 icons centered around the theme of growth, inspired by my belief in the transformative power of solitude and nature. I find personal growth through two key spaces: quiet, reflective moments in nature and meaningful, interactive connections within the community. Both of these realms contribute equally to my sense of balance and well-being. I chose natural colours you would find in a beautiful green spaces to reflect peace and tranquility. I kept the colours muted and also used brushstrokes that were soft ensuring the designs remained gentle and organic. 

--- 

Assignment 3 - 3D Design 

I unified the projects concept under the theme of 'Identity,' structuring the work into three distinct stages that explore different facets of personal growth. Working in 3D design offered a completely new creative experience, different from flat design that required a new approach and process to be successful. I was inspired by textures and shapes, allowing me to be precise with the details for each space. The first-person perspective creates an immersive experience, drawing the viewer directly into my world. The use of a 'yellow brick road' allows the user to follow the journey while symbolizing self-exploration and the discovery of myself and my community. These stages are linked through  visual and audio elements, creating a cohesiveness to the narrative upon exploration.
