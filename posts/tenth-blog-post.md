**Assignment 1 - Video Edit**
--- 
**How did you approach the relationship between the sonic and visual components of
your edit?**   

My video concept was based around the dynamics of my life and the juxtaposition of a calm and quiet side to a busy, public side. When adding sound, i wanted the audio to enhance the visual elements by matching a softer sound with the quiet side and then something high energy to show the change. Pairing the visual and audio like this really helped tell the story in a clear way. 

**How did you decide on the footage that you used to shape your project?**

I honestly just filmed what i do in a week, 50% of the time i like to be by myself, either just chilling or going for a walk or studying / working, and the other half im with my Ballroom family/community training, peforming or planning events. 

**What did you learn about the power of sound to shape emotional experience, and how
did you apply this knowledge to your project?** 

Sound allows you to enhance emotions in so many ways. It can establish moods, spaces and ideas, it can reinforce whatever is happening visually or even contradict the visual elements to evoke uncertainty or suspense. All of this adds an incredible ammount of emotions and value the video. It can be a language in itself and for this project i chose to really lean into usuing sound to reinforce my visuals. I also used J and L cuts to tease or connect upcoming visuals with the sound. 

**How does editing relate to the concepts of pacing and rhythm, and how did you use
these concepts to shape your use of time in the project?**

Pacing of the video is vital as it dictates the overall speed or tempo of the video and how the story progresses. Essentially you are creating a sense of time. 
I used short, quick cuts to create a fast-paced feel for the peformance side of my video and keep the energy lively. 
I slowed down the pace for the reflective scenes by keeping them longer and uses pauses in the filming. I made use of the empty space and almost bland space in the visuals to help them manage the pacing. 


**Which parts of your personality, interests, past, and potential future did you choose to
depict in your portrait?**

I chose to show my personal side and who i am as *sam* and also show my passion for queer culture and community, specifically in a Ballroom context. Ballroom has become a huge part of my purpose and has helped shape my identity. I see Ballroom continuing to play a large and significant role in my future and i hope to one day be able to give back to the community in the ways its given to me and help keep the culture growing so the young queer youth can find Ballroom and what it has to offer. 


**What were the primary aesthetic choices that you made to express those ideas in your
work?** 

Aesthetically i chose to keep the action shots more close up to intensify the rhythmn and focus of the viewer and kept the other shots wider and slower allowing the viewer to soak up the scenery and stillness in the visuals.
