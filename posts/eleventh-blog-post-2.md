# Finalising my layout / design

---

![wf](wf.png)

After playing around with a lot of different compositions i likes these two wireframes i drafted up, i went on to base my website layout on these two, switching it up per project to keep it refreshing but have an overall theme. 

![figma1](figma1.png)

![figma2](figma2.png)

I tried having the boxes/strokes on the outside of the frames, but i didnt like the way it was looking and feeling. So i shifted away a bit from the original concept and chose to leave the frames bare (still in the right positions) but no outline. I like the open, floating feel it creates upon the blue background. To me the background colour is meant to represent skies, space, even the ocean - big spaces that make me feel so small. Whenever i am overwhelemed, or im at the beach, i find such peace in being reminded just how tiny i am in this world, the day/night skies and open waters always remind me of this and bring me back down to earth and i love it so much. 

![figma3](figma3.png)

I think the website looks so mcuh better than how i started it, as everything has a home and is wokring in tandem with the image or text above or below it. It flows well, makes sense and is visually cohesive. I also chose to break up my artist statements so it flows as you read/process the info and visuals. Again i wanted the images and texts to be floating like they are in water or outterspace. I tested having the text in one main paragraph, but it felt too sudden for the web page concept.
