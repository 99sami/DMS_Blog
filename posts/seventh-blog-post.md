# Assignment 3 - Crafting a 3D World that Reflects Identity: A Journey through Three Stages


For assignment 3, the concept of using a 3D world to represent personal identity is an exciting way to merge creativity with story. By breaking it into three stages, I chose to symbolize the immense growth / transformation / self-discovery journey I went on after moving out of my home city to melbourne.


I moved to melbourne in January 2020, just before we entered a pandemic and COVID took over our lives for a few years. The excitement that was just starting to brew of being in a new, big city at 19 soon boiled down to stress, anxiety and loneliness in the intense lockdowns Melbourne had. Although at the time I couldn't help but be sad that my new journey took such an unexpected turn, and that I would spend the next year or two inside my sharehouse, it was actually the biggest blessing in disguise. Lockdown allowed myself to truly sit with myself and develop my identity. A lot of my life in Canberra I wasn't living authentically, and I would have just taken that to Melbourne without the time lockdown allowing me to spend with myself and learn about who i am and who I want to become. I emerged from lockdown a completely different person and immediately aligned with people who I now call my chosen family. I found ballroom and I found my community that allowed me to be myself. I'm grateful for the time I had to reflect during the stillness of lockdown, and although it felt lonely not having my family and friends, I became my own best friend.
