# Assignment 3 - Crafting a 3D World that Reflects Identity: A Journey through Three Stages

For assignment 3, the concept of using a 3D world to represent personal identity is an exciting way to merge creativity with story. By breaking it into three stages, i chose to symboolise the immense growth / transformation / self-discovery journey i went on after moving out of my home city to melbourne. 

I moved to melbourne in January 2020, just before we entered a pandemic and COVID took over our lives for a few years. The excitment that was just starting to brew of being in a new, big city at 19 soon boiled down to stress, anxiety and lonliness in the intense lockdowns melbourne had. Although at the time i couldnt help but be sad that my new journey took such an unexpected turn, and that i would spend the next year or two inside my sharehouse, it was actually the biggest blessing in disguise. Lockdown allowed myself to truly sit with myself and develop my identity. A lot of my life in canberra i wasnt living authenticly, and i wouldve just taken that to melbourne without the time lockdown allowed me to spend with myself and learn about who i am and who i want to become. I emerged out of lockdown a completly different person and immediatly aligned with people who i now call my chosen family. I found ballroom and i found my community that allowed me to be myself. Im grateful for the time i had to reflect during the stillness of lockdown, and although it felt lonely not having my family and friends, i became my own bestfriend.
